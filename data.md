# ABOUT THE CLUB

## What is The 1% Club?
The **1% Club** is a student-driven skill development and employability enhancement club established to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry expectations. Through workshops, competitions, industry interactions, peer-learning sessions, and practical activities, the club empowers students to develop the professional skills required for academic success, employability, and leadership excellence.

## Why The 1% Club?
The name **"1% Club"** is inspired by the philosophy of continuous improvement. Small improvements made consistently every day lead to remarkable personal and professional growth over time. The club encourages students to become **1% better every day** through learning, practice, reflection, and application.
